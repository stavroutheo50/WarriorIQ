    How points work
    • +{{ points_rules.session }} for each training session you upload for one of your missions: at least a minute of you doing the drill. Up to {{ points_rules.daily }} sessions a day earn points.
    • +{{ points_rules.done }} when you finish a mission you uploaded at least one session for.
    • +{{ points_rules.improved }} when your next analysed fight shows a mission's number actually improved: the tick on its bar.
    • Every 100 points is a level, and levels earn a rank: Rookie, Prospect (level 3), Contender (5), Challenger (8) and Champion (12). Your streak is the weeks in a row with at least one counted session; {{ standing.week_target }} a week is a full week.
    • {{ points_rules.redeem_cost }} points buy one extra analysis, up to {{ points_rules.redeem_per_month }} a month. It is used when your plan's own analyses for the day or month run out, and given back if the analysis fails. Spending points never lowers your level. Points cannot be bought or turned into money.
    • The upload check sees that a real video shows someone moving. It cannot tell who, or which drill, which is why the big points only come from your next fight. Clips are deleted as soon as they are checked.
