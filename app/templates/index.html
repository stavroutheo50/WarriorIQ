What a coach sees from the corner.

Four things decide most rounds before anyone counts a punch. WarriorIQ watches all four for both fighters, then builds a four-week plan from your own rounds{% if features.scoring.on %}, with a round-by-round score estimate{% endif %}.{% if not features.scoring.on %} Score estimates are coming.{% endif %}

  • Movement

    How much ground each fighter covers, and who owns the centre of the ring.

  • Pressure

    Who walks forward, who gets backed up, and when the round turned.

  • Guard

    How often the hands stay up, and how long they drop after an exchange.

  • Balance

    How steady each stance stays, and what happens to your feet after you throw.

Three steps. One phone.

  1. 1

    Film it

    Side-on, both fighters head to feet. A phone on the ropes or a tripod is enough.

  2. 2

    Tap who is who

    One tap on each fighter. WarriorIQ follows them through the whole video.

  3. 3

    Train on it

    Read your report round by round and take one clear thing into your next session.

Is WarriorIQ an official judge?

No. {% if features.scoring.on %}Scorecards are evidence-gated estimates for training and review, not replacements for licensed officials.{% else %}WarriorIQ does not score fights yet; when score estimates arrive they will be training estimates, not a replacement for licensed officials.{% endif %}

Can I analyse a fight without an account?

No, but the account is free: the button at the top of this page creates one and takes you straight to the upload. A fight belongs to a workspace — your footage, the athletes in it and your upload allowance are all attached to an account — so WarriorIQ asks you to create one first. It is free, and your fights stay private to you.

Can under-18s use it?

Yes: accounts are open to every age. Under {{request.state.minimum_account_age}}, a parent or guardian approves the account by email before any fight is uploaded. Junior athletes can appear in footage only when the uploader has all required rights and appropriate parent or guardian permission.

How accurate are the strike counts?

Not accurate enough to treat as exact. They are labelled as rough estimates everywhere they appear; the accuracy checks are why.

What happens when the AI is uncertain?

A quick quality check catches hard-to-see footage first. After that, the analysis leaves out anything the video cannot support and says so on the report. When it cannot tell the two fighters apart, the report asks you to point them out again on a clear picture.
