Questions

Before you upload

Is WarriorIQ an official judge?

No. {% if features.scoring.on %}Scorecards are evidence-gated estimates for training and review, not replacements for licensed officials.{% else %}WarriorIQ does not score fights yet; when score estimates arrive they will be training estimates, not a replacement for licensed officials.{% endif %}

Can I analyse a fight without an account?

No. A fight belongs to a workspace — your footage, the athletes in it and your upload allowance are all attached to an account — so WarriorIQ asks you to create one first. It is free, and your fights stay private to you.

How accurate are the strike counts?

Not accurate enough to treat as exact. They are labelled as rough estimates everywhere they appear; the accuracy checks are why.

Can I see a report before signing up?

Yes. The sample report shows the layout with made-up fighters and numbers.

What happens when the AI is uncertain?

It leaves out anything the video cannot support and says so on the report. When it cannot tell the two fighters apart, the report asks you to point them out again on a clear picture.
