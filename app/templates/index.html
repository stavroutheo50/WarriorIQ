Questions

Before you upload

Is WarriorIQ an official judge?

No. Scorecards are evidence-gated estimates for training and review, not replacements for licensed officials.

Does a guest fight remain saved?

No. Guest files are held temporarily so the report can work, excluded from the fight library, and automatically removed after two hours.

What happens when the AI is uncertain?

It uses recovery evidence and suppresses unsupported claims. The planned uncertainty flow asks only a few simple questions when automatic recovery cannot resolve an important moment.
