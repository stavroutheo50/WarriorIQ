Accuracy

How accurate it is, measured.

These are the checks done so far, on small sets of footage. They are findings, not validated accuracy figures, and they are why counts are labelled as estimates.

Flagged actions

On 178 clips from three reference fights, checked by hand, about a third of the actions the report displayed were real strikes.

One labelled bout

On a Kick Light bout labelled by a competitor, 11 of 16 counted moments were real strikes, and 6 of those had the right type.

Against official stats

On three UFC rounds, strikes thrown while both fighters stood were close to the official numbers (29 vs 21, 36 vs 12, 20 vs 17). In grappling the counts ran three to six times too high.

Guard

Not yet checked against hand-labelled clips, so it is marked experimental wherever it appears.

Strike counts are labelled as estimates on every report for this reason. Movement, centre, pressure and balance come from body position and are not affected; guard stays marked experimental until it is checked.
